Mage Enhancements

Adds the Sorcerer's Reliquary: a single accessory that combines the effects of the
Arcane Flower, Magnet Flower, Celestial Cuffs, Celestial Emblem and Mana Cloak.

In one slot it grants:
- Reduced mana usage and automatic Mana Potion use
- Increased mana regeneration
- +20 maximum mana and mana restored when you take damage
- Increased Star pickup range
- Falling stars when you take damage
- 15% increased magic damage

Craft it at a Tinkerer's Workbench from any 3 of those 5 accessories.
