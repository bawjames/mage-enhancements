Mage Enhancements

Adds the Sorcerer's Reliquary: a single accessory that combines the effects of the
Arcane Flower, Magnet Flower, Celestial Cuffs, Celestial Emblem and Mana Cloak.

In one slot it grants:
- Reduces mana usage by 8% and automatically uses Mana Potions
- Increases maximum mana by 20 and restores mana when damaged
- 15% increased magic damage
- 8% increased magic critical strike chance
- Increased Star pickup range
- Falling stars when you take damage
- Reduced enemy aggression

Craft it at a Tinkerer's Workbench from any 3 different ones of those 5 accessories.
